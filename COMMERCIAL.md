# Commercial licensing

Casino Suite is free for non-commercial projects (with visible credit, see [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md)).
If you make money with it — directly or indirectly — you need one of the licenses below.
Prices are **one-time per product** (one game, app or website), perpetual, and include 12 months of updates.

| Tier | Who it's for | Price (one-time, per product) | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Indie** | Individuals and companies with < €100,000 annual revenue or funding | **€ 290** | All four games · commercial use in one product · credit optional |
| **Studio** | Companies with < €2 million annual revenue | **€ 1,490** | Everything in Indie · credit can be removed · rebranding / reskinning allowed · priority issue support |
| **Enterprise / White-label** | Larger companies, agencies building for multiple clients, platforms | **from € 4,900** | Multiple products · white-label rights · support agreement · custom terms |
| **Operator** | Any real-money, crypto or prize gambling | **on request** (fixed fee or revenue share) | Written permission for gambling use · you remain responsible for certification and gaming licenses |

**Fair-use promises**

- **No surprise audits and no royalties** for Indie, Studio and Enterprise — you pay once per product.
- **Grow-into pricing:** if you outgrow Indie, you only pay the difference to Studio.
- **Past use is fixable:** found out late that your use is commercial? Buy the right tier and you're covered, no penalties.
- **Education & non-profits:** free, also when a school or charity runs it publicly — just keep the credit.
- **Unsure?** Ask before you ship. A short description of your project is enough.

## How to get a license

Open an issue in this repository titled **"Commercial license – <your project>"** (you can keep details private and ask for contact by e-mail),
or contact the author via the GitHub profile [fiveworld-development](https://github.com/fiveworld-development).
You will receive an invoice and a written license that names your product.

*Prices exclude VAT where applicable.*
